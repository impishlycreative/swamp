Core concept: reinterpretation

Reveal Reframe

What you are learning: A good reveal can change the meaning of details already on the page instead of merely adding new information. You will begin a scene under one assumption, receive a reveal, and continue without deleting what you already wrote.

Your scene stays in your own document or notebook.
