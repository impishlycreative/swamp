Core concept: constrained writing

Constraint

Goal: Solve a writing problem while obeying a craft restriction. The restriction is not there to make the writing “correct”; it forces you to notice the choices you normally make automatically and find alternatives.

Choose a scenario or let the game pick one. Write outside the page; the game supplies the situation and rule.

How to play

Write the scene for 5–10 minutes without breaking the rule. If you get stuck, do not remove the rule. Change your technique.

Reflection: What did the restriction force you to do differently? Which replacement technique worked best?
