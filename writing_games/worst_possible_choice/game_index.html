Core concept: believable bad decisions

Worst Possible Choice

Goal: Make a damaging decision feel psychologically inevitable enough that the reader understands it. You will build motivation, add pressure, commit to the bad choice, then face a consequence caused by that choice.

Situation

Choose the trouble

Stage 0 of 4 · Choose a bad decision
Pick one bad choice. You will not be allowed to switch after the pressure starts.

Completion test

The choice should make sense because of what the character wants, fears, believes, misunderstands, or refuses to lose. Do not rescue the character by changing the decision once the pressure becomes uncomfortable.
