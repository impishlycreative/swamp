Swamp Writing Games

Short writing games built to teach by doing. Most take about five minutes, work solo or alongside a group, and never ask you to enter your writing into the website.

How to use them: Pick a concept you want to practise, open the game, read the short learning blurb, and do the writing in your own notebook or document. Completion matters; winning does not.
