Core concept: conflict through competing goals

Character Collision

Goal: Create conflict from what two characters want, value, or fear—not just from arguing. Both characters should have understandable reasons, and the shared situation should make easy compromise difficult.

How to play

Write the scene without making either character obviously foolish or evil. Let each pursue what they want. The conflict should emerge because satisfying one makes satisfying the other harder.

Reflection: If one character simply vanished, would the central conflict disappear? If not, the conflict may still be coming from the plot rather than the relationship.
