Ambush

Adapt a scene when a credible complication attacks one of its assumptions. The point is not surprise for its own sake; the point is causal adaptation.

Debrief

  • Which assumption did the ambush attack?
  • What did the characters now have to do differently?
  • What earlier detail made the complication believable?
  • Did the scene gain pressure, change direction, or merely become noisier?
  • What consequence should carry into the next scene?
Design rule: the complication must grow from the story already in motion. It should change the problem, not simply add random trouble.
