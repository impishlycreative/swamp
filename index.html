Writing Games

Short writing games built to teach by doing. Most take about five minutes, work solo or alongside a group, and keep your actual writing in your own notebook or document.

How to use them: Pick a skill you want to practise, open the game, read the learning goal, and write outside the site. The games provide prompts, pressure, structure, and constraints; they do not judge your prose.
