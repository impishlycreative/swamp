Core concept: causal plotting with decreasing support

Cause & Effect

Goal: Build a scene where each beat happens because of an earlier action or decision. The game gradually stops supplying answers until you are constructing the causal chain yourself.

Starting decision
Round 1 of 4 · Guided consequence

Causality test

After every beat, ask: Can this result be traced to something a character did, chose, failed to do, or caused? If the connection only works as “and then,” strengthen the bridge.
