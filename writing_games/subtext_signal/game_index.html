Core concept: subtext

Subtext Signal

What you are learning: Dialogue becomes richer when characters speak about one thing while wanting, fearing, or hiding another. Write a short exchange that lets the hidden motive shape wording, evasion, politeness, and pressure without stating the motive outright.

Your dialogue stays in your own notebook or document.

Relationship
Surface topic
Speaker A secretly wants
Speaker B secretly wants
Round constraint

Write 4–6 lines of dialogue. Neither character may directly state the hidden motive.
