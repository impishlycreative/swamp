Core concept: goal, obstacle & conflict

Goal Under Pressure

Goal: Build a scene around a character actively trying to achieve something while meaningful obstacles interfere. Pressure should force new tactics and decisions rather than simply delay success.

How to play

Write the character actively pursuing the goal. After each pressure card, make the character change tactics, accept a cost, or choose between priorities. Do not let them simply wait for the obstacle to disappear.

Reflection: Did the character drive the scene through choices? If the same events would happen while they did nothing, strengthen their agency.
