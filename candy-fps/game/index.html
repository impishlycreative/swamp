Candy FPS

Find the pieces of the candy key. Face the sweets that come alive after moonrise. Free your sibling.

Touch: left stick to move, drag the scene to aim, hold FIRE to shoot, tap the glove to punch. Craft anywhere. Landscape recommended.

Desktop: WASD, click the scene for mouse look, left click to fire, F to punch, Q to swap, C to craft, Esc to release the mouse.

Three prototype missions; original procedural art and campaign-boundary autosaves. Internet is required to load Babylon.js. Design plan
