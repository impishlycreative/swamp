Core concept: conflict through competing goals

Character Collision

Goal: Create conflict from two characters pursuing outcomes that cannot both happen. A collision is not simply two different personalities or private preferences. The opposition must be active: each character must know about, threaten, block, or force action around the other character's goal.

How to play

Write both characters as reasonable people actively pursuing their own outcome. Do not solve the scene by making one suddenly agree, behave foolishly, or stop caring.

Collision test: Can both characters get exactly what they want at the same time? If yes, there is no real collision.

Active-conflict test: Is one character actually blocking, threatening, exposing, withholding, controlling, or forcing a choice for the other? A hidden motive by itself is not enough.

Reflection: Which character forced the other to change tactics, accept a cost, or make a harder choice?
