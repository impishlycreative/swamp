Goal Under Pressure

Goal: Keep one character actively pursuing one clear outcome while pressure attacks that goal in a deliberate sequence: time/resource pressure, tactic failure, relationship complication, then a goal-vs-cost choice.

Begin with the goal and obstacle. Make the character take an active first tactic.

Completion test

The character should change tactics at least twice. The last pressure must force a choice that costs something; it should not merely add one more inconvenience.
