Core concept: complication & reversal

Ambush

Goal: Keep a scene coherent when an unexpected complication changes the situation. The surprise should affect what the character wants, what stands in the way, or what their next choice costs.

Choose one of 10 starting scenarios or let the game pick one. Write wherever you normally write; the game supplies the pressure.
