Group Writing Games

These games require people to work together on one writing problem. Players may control fictional characters who disagree, but the players themselves are always cooperating. There are no opposing teams, traitors, saboteurs, or player-versus-player win conditions.

What makes a game belong here: each player must hold something the others need—information, responsibility, a constraint, or part of the story state—and the shared result should be weaker or impossible if one person simply tries to do everything alone.
