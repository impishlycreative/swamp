Subtext Signal — Goal and Examples Review

Review method
Vale → Skeptic → Professor → Continuity Auditor → Editor → Vale reconciliation.

Goal
The game is meant to teach dialogue subtext: characters talk about one surface subject while hidden wants, fears, suspicions, or needs shape what they say and do not say.

How the mechanic is supposed to meet the goal
Each setup provides a relationship, a concrete surface topic, and a different hidden objective for each speaker. The writer then produces a short exchange without allowing either character to state the hidden motive directly. Three changing constraints force the writer to use different subtext tactics.

How the examples support the goal
The bank contains 10 paired setups and 10 dialogue constraints, producing well over 30 meaningful exercise combinations. The surface topics are concrete enough to keep the scene grounded: an old box, a starting position, a tree branch, borrowing a car, a difficult client, a missing item, attending a party, an unfinished assignment, a costume problem, and a family visit.

The hidden objectives create actual tension beneath those topics. One sibling wants the box for sentimental reasons while the other wants to leave before a difficult memory surfaces. A coach wants reassurance while a player may be considering quitting. A customer wants to avoid suspicion while a shop owner wants to learn whether the customer took something.

The constraints then turn subtext into a craft action rather than an abstract idea: changing the subject, becoming unusually polite, answering questions with questions, using an object as distraction, saying something technically true but misleading, joking instead of being honest, repeating a phrase, or changing specificity.

Review
This game meets its goal well because the examples distinguish surface conversation from underlying agenda and give the learner concrete tactics for expressing that gap.

The main ambiguity is whether each new constraint is supposed to continue the same exchange or produce a fresh version of it. For learning purposes, rewriting the same setup under three different tactics is the cleaner interpretation because it makes the effect of technique easier to compare.

Verdict
PASS. The example system provides more than 30 meaningful combinations and directly practices subtext rather than merely describing it.
