# Review notes

## Vale
The game is built around adapting a scene rather than merely reacting to a random prompt.

## Skeptic
The timer is tension, not the lesson. The learning depends on whether the ambush attacks an established assumption and forces a meaningful change. Avoid turning the story-state display into a fake quality score.

## Professor
The learning objective is causal adaptation under changed story conditions. The debrief requires the group to identify setup, changed conditions, and persistent consequence so the exercise transfers back to scene construction.

## Editor
Each included scenario contains an explicit goal, pressure, assumption, and causal ambush. Ambushes are tied to prior facts rather than unrelated disasters. Scenario structures vary across professional, family, performance, ethical, and travel pressures rather than simply changing nouns.
