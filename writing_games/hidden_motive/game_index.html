Core concept: motivation & subtext

Hidden Motive

Goal: Let an unstated motive shape what a character does and says. The hidden motive should change behavior; it should not merely be a secret fact waiting to be revealed.

Choose situation
Visible situation
Visible goal
Private motive
Reveal when ready.

How to play

Write the scene while pursuing the visible goal. Once you reveal the private motive, continue without directly explaining it. Let it affect word choice, avoidance, attention, body language, priorities, or decisions.

Reflection: Could a reader feel that something else was driving the character before being told what it was?
