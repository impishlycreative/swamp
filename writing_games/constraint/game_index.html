Core concept: increasing craft restriction

Constraint

Goal: Solve one scene four times as the game removes familiar techniques and forces deliberate substitutes. The restriction should make you choose another craft tool, not merely make the scene awkward.

Scene
Skill focus
Stage 1 of 4 · Single restriction

Completion test

At the end, identify the technique that replaced the forbidden one. If the scene simply became thinner, the constraint did not teach enough.
