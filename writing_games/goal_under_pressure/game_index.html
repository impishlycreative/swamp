Core concept: goal, obstacle & conflict

Goal Under Pressure

Goal: Build a scene around a character actively trying to achieve something while an obstacle directly prevents that outcome. Each new pressure should attack the same goal and force a new tactic, cost, or decision.

How to play

Write the character actively pursuing the goal. After each pressure card, make the character change tactics, accept a cost, or choose between priorities. Do not let the obstacle become irrelevant.

Conflict test: Does this obstacle make the stated goal harder right now? If removing the obstacle would not materially change the scene, the conflict is too weak.

Reflection: Did the character drive the scene through choices? If the same events would happen while they did nothing, strengthen their agency.
