Core concept: selective description

Sensory Lens

What you are learning: Description gets stronger when details are chosen for a purpose instead of listed because they exist. Each round gives you a different lens plus one odd detail; your job is to decide whether that detail deserves to survive.

Do the writing in your own notebook or document.
