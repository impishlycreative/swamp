Core concept: character motivation & dilemma

Worst Possible Choice

Goal: Make a bad decision feel believable. Good characters do not need to make smart choices, but their choices should make sense because of what they want, fear, believe, misunderstand, or refuse to lose.

Choose one of 10 situations, then select a bad choice and write the scene that makes it emotionally and logically plausible.

Do not rescue the character by changing the choice. Instead, establish enough motivation that a reader can say, “I understand why they did it,” even if they know it is a mistake.
