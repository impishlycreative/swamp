Core concept: motivation becoming subtext

Hidden Motive

Goal: Let a private motive alter behaviour without turning into a confession. You begin with only the visible goal, then learn the motive, face pressure, and finally make the motive leak through wording, attention, body language, avoidance, or choice.

Completion test

Before the motive is ever stated, a reader should be able to sense that the character is being driven by something beyond the visible goal.
