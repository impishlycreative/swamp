Core concept: reinterpretation through new information

Revelation

Goal: Introduce new information that changes the meaning of what is already on the page. A useful revelation should make the reader reinterpret an earlier action, relationship, object, or assumption—not simply add a surprise from nowhere.

Choose starting situation
Starting situation
Revelation
Write the opening first. Reveal when ready.

How to play

Write the opening situation as though your character understands what is happening. Then reveal the new information. Continue without deleting the earlier material. Make something already established take on a different meaning.

Reflection: What changed meaning after the reveal? If nothing earlier reads differently, the revelation may just be an extra event.
