A first-person horror atmosphere prototype · v0.6.0

There's a road.
Somewhere, a town.

It is after midnight. The moon is full. The grass is moving, and the crickets sound close enough to touch. You are already holding a lantern.

Tap or click to walk; double-tap or double-click to run. One tap while running switches to walking toward a new destination. Drag to look, Shift + WASD to run on desktop, right-click to lock mouselook. Pattycaker appears near the town limits, or use Show Pattycaker to test her now. Other controls reveal ghosts, shadows, and rustles. Recorded child voices require audio clips. Sound requires interaction. This is a procedural WebGL prototype. Use Export Pattycaker GLB to save a reusable skinned rig with basic animation clips; it remains prototype-quality art.
